Link Opener

Paste any text, like a WhatsApp chat export. The page finds every link in it and opens each one in a new tab.

    The first time you click "Open all", Chrome may block every tab after the first one. Click the pop-up icon at the right of the address bar, choose Always allow pop-ups for this site, and click "Open all" again. Auto-open needs that setting too, because its later runs happen without a click.

    Auto-open runs only while this tab stays open and the computer is awake. After the laptop wakes up, it opens the links once and then goes back to the normal schedule.
